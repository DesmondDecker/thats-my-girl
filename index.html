Why we exist

The talent is here. The doors should be too.

Girls and women in tech face pressure to succeed, feeling left out, low confidence and discrimination. Many don’t know where to start, or where to find the people and opportunities that could change their path.

  • Overwhelmed choosing between AI, design, photography or coding
  • Feeling like you don’t belong in tech
  • Few women to look up to, and few doors open
  • Founders building alone, without mentors or a network
